# 1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) ek aisi technology hai jo machines ko **human intelligence** ki tarah sochne, samajhne aur decision lene ki capability deti hai.

Simple language me:

> AI ka goal hai computer ko itna intelligent banana ki wo human ki tarah problems solve kar sake.

Example:

Normally agar tum calculator use karte ho to wo sirf predefined formula follow karta hai.

Lekin agar tum ChatGPT se pucho:

```
Mujhe Java interview ke liye prepare karao.
```

To AI sirf predefined answer nahi deta.

Wo:

- Question samajhta hai
- Context analyze karta hai
- Previous knowledge use karta hai
- New response generate karta hai

Yehi Artificial Intelligence hai.
